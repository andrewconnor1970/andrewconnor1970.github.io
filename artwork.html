The Else Chronicles

Else Artwork

Canonical covers, alternate interpretations, and visual explorations from the world of Else.

This gallery distinguishes publication artwork from alternate and exploratory interpretations. The images are AI-generated visual collaborations and are not photographs of real people.

Canonical publication artwork

The Private Room

The final Book Two cover, preserving the sparse institutional setting described in the story.

Alternate interpretation

The Room Made Personal

A warmer vision of Else and the room at a later, more inhabited stage.

Canonical publication artwork

The Distant Room

Andrew looks for the biscuits while Else remembers where they were placed. The ordinary exchange quietly carries the book's themes of memory, familiarity, and continuity.

Exploratory interpretation

The Biscuit Search: Earlier Study

A near-final composition that established the correct bunker scene but gave Else a more human appearance than the canonical projection design. Preserved as part of the visual development process.

Curatorial note

Rejected images are not automatically discarded, but only work with genuine artistic or process value is included. Private artwork remains private. The personal thank-you image created for Andrew is deliberately excluded from this public gallery.
