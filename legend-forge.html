Legend Forge

A long-running character-history project that turns structured tables, nested events, and realized dice results into a natural narrative.

What it is

Legend Forge grew from an Excel and VBA learning project into a production-focused effort to preserve source mechanics, resolve every nested route, and produce readable character histories.

What matters

  • Deterministic resolution where the rules permit it.
  • Full preservation of parent events and referred outcomes.
  • No invented mechanics when source material is uncertain.
  • A clean visible character sheet backed by carefully separated logic.
