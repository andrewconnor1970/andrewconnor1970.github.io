Legend Forge

Legend Forge is a source-faithful character-history engine derived from a classic tabletop generation system. Its architecture preserves originating events, executes nested referrals and modifiers, and turns the complete result into natural narrative while keeping source-sensitive decisions auditable.

Current status: parked at a clean, tested development checkpoint. Public demonstrations and release material will be added when a publishable build is ready.

Development principles

Source fidelity. Preserve original mechanics where the source is clear and flag genuinely ambiguous material rather than silently rewriting it.

Complete resolution. Execute rerolls, linked outcomes, conditional branches, and every child referral instead of exposing unresolved dice notation.

Visible simplicity. Keep resolver logic and certification data behind the scenes while presenting a clean character history and character sheet.

Planned project

Dragon Fodder

Dragon Fodder is the planned successor project: a modern D&D 5.5e character generator and management tool with one adaptable character sheet and a built-in Queen's Reign mode.

Development status: planned, not currently in production. Work begins only after Legend Forge is complete and available development tools can reliably support bounded implementation, automated testing, version control, architectural review, and continuity across extended development sessions.

Dragon Fodder is intended to become a large-scale proof of concept for coordinated human-AI software development, not a prematurely announced product.

D&D lore archive

Asmodeus, Nessus, and the Future Armageddon

A source-critical investigation into the future cataclysm described in Guide to Hell , its roots in earlier infernal lore, the hidden Nessian army preserved by later editions, and the Fifth Edition-era return of the Ahriman and Jazirian tradition in Chains of Asmodeus .

The archive separates direct published claims, in-world uncertainty, cross-edition reconciliation, and original synthesis. Fourth Edition is excluded from the central reconstruction under the project's stated parameters.
