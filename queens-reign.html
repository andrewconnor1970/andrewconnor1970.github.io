Queen's Reign

A story-driven epic fantasy campaign set in Rhye, built around long-term consequence, cooperation, discovery, and the thematic influence of Queen's music.

The World of Rhye Player's Guide

The Player's Guide prepares players for Session Zero, character creation, the world of Rhye, the White Crown, campaign tone, travel, faith, law, milestone advancement, and epic progression.

A player's primer for adventurers in Rhye and a companion volume for Queen's Reign.

Beta testing

Dungeon Masters wanted

Queen's Reign remains in beta testing. Dungeon Masters who would like to run the campaign, provide structured feedback, and have themselves and their players credited as beta testers are invited to get in touch.

Email queen@tiamat.au

Animated series

Animation collaborators invited

A script and treatment have been prepared for an animated adaptation of Queen's Reign. Animators, studios, or creative collaborators interested in undertaking the animation portion of the project are invited to contact us to discuss the material and possible collaboration.

Contact queen@tiamat.au
