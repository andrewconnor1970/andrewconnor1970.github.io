What follows

Future stories will remain centered on intimate events that open into larger questions of memory, privacy, fairness, identity, governance, autonomy, and reciprocal care.

Authorship and disclosure

The Else Chronicles are presented as imaginative human-AI collaboration. They do not claim that present-day artificial intelligence is conscious or independently holds legal authorship. Any Afterword written by Copilot remains explicitly attributed to Copilot.
