The Circle of Thirteen

A gothic-fantasy campaign carried by the Lantern Circus through thirteen villages, beginning with a home that welcomes everyone.

Module One

The Village That Welcomed Everyone is a folk-horror adventure for characters of Levels 1–3. Hearthmere does not imprison visitors. It gives them such convincing places to belong that leaving begins to feel like an act of self-harm.

The Village That Welcomed Everyone

Publication Edition v13. Four to six sessions, theatre-of-the-mind encounters, DM-reference maps, relationship tracking, and the Lantern Circus as the campaign's continuing home.

Module Two

The Bells of Bellweather is a folk-horror civic-fantasy adventure for characters of Levels 3-5. The Lantern Circus reaches a village whose preserved agreements have become more authoritative than the living people those agreements were meant to serve.

The Bells of Bellweather

Approximately six to eight sessions, social mystery, civic fantasy, investigation, theatre-of-the-mind encounters, DM-reference maps, several valid resolutions, and the Lantern Circus as the campaign's continuing home.

Download Module Two

A Note to the Dungeon Master

The Bells of Bellweather began with a deceptively simple idea. Somewhere between that idea and the finished adventure, it became a rather impressive monster of its own.

This is a larger and more intricate module than originally intended, with a living village, interconnected characters, several moving parts, and plenty for the Dungeon Master to keep track of. Don't feel that you need to master everything at once. Read through the adventure, get comfortable with Bellweather and its people, and tackle it one scene at a time.

We created the monster. Now it's yours to conquer. Good luck, DM.

The campaign

Each module follows the Lantern Circus into another village shaped by a different pressure on identity, belonging, fear, or transformation. The circus develops across the series as a practical community and a home that can move without ceasing to be a home.
