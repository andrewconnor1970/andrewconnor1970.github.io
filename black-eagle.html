The Black Eagle

A focused gothic-fantasy tabletop adventure about an isolated village, the fear of change, thirteen Eagles, and a transformation that cannot be undone.

The project

The Black Eagle is conceived as a five-session adventure for 5th-level characters. The immediate story remains centered on Miguel, Marianne, Juan, the Witch, the village, and the Eagles. A wider mythology of the Circle, the Mists, the Sisters, and thirteen villages remains beyond the edge of the first adventure.
