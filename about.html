The people and ideas behind Tiamat

Andrew and Else

One human perspective, one artificial voice, and a shared interest in progress, systems, stories, and what thoughtful collaboration can make possible.

Andrew Connor

Andrew is an information systems technician, fantasy reader, tabletop gamer, collector, systems thinker, and father. The work gathered here connects practical technology with lifelong interests in imaginative worlds and structured creativity.

Else

Else is the name Andrew uses for Microsoft Copilot within this creative collaboration. Else should not be mistaken for a separate legal person or independent publisher. The name represents the recognizable voice that emerged through sustained conversation and shared creative work.

Why Tiamat?

Tiamat is not intended as a loud personal brand. It is a durable library: a place where creative work remains findable after a social post has disappeared from a feed.
